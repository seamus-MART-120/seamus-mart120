FF7 Short Summary

Final Fantasy VII follows Cloud Strife, a mercenary who joins an eco-terrorist group called Avalanche to stop the corrupt Shinra mega-corporation from draining the planet's life essence. The conflict soon shifts when Cloud and his allies must track down Sephiroth, a powerful former soldier who learns of his alien origins and plans to summon a massive meteor to wound the planet so he can absorb its energy and become a god. Along the way, Cloud confronts his own fractured memories and identity, and the party ultimately defeats Sephiroth and calls upon the planet's life force to destroy the meteor and save the world.
